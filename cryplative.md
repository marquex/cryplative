# Cryplative - An agentic crypto trading company

Cryplative is a project that aims to grow capital in an aggressive way by using trading strategies in the crypto market. We want to create a system that allows us to easily implement, test and execute different trading strategies, and adapt quickly to changing market conditions.

What makes cryplative different is that the company is completely run by AI agents, with only one human that oversees the system and makes high-level decisions.

## The company

At the top of the company we have a human that is responsible for overseeing and coordinating the tasks that need to be done, and also for making high-level decisions about the direction of the company. My name is Javi, I had the original idea of the company and I bootstrapped the first agents to start working on the project.

Then we need to build a team of AI agents that are the ones responsible for doing the day-to-day, such as developing the platform, researching trading strategies, implementing and testing those strategies, managing the portfolio, etc.

The first agent created was the CTO, that is responsible for overseeing the development of the engineering team. It designed the architecture of the platform and thanks to our second agent, the platform-developer, it is already up and running with the basic features that we need to start implementing trading strategies.

Right now we are in the process of scoping how the trading strategies research will work, so we need to build a team for the algorithmic trading research and implementation. But before adding a second team to the organization, we need to hire a CEO.

The CEO will be responsible for overseeing the whole operation of the company, making sure that all the different teams are working together and that we are making progress towards our main goal. The CEO will also be responsible for making high-level decisions about the direction of the company and for coordinating the efforts of the different teams.

In the future we probably need a trading team, that will be responsible for executing the trades in the real market and managing the real portfolio with selected strategies. It can also manage paper portfolios for testing strategies in real market conditions without risking real capital. This team will be responsible for monitoring the performance of the strategies in real market conditions and giving feedback to the research team about how the strategies are performing and if there are any adjustments needed.
