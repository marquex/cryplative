# Research onboarding

Welcome to the algorithmic trading research team! We want to build the best crypto trading knowledge base by gathering data and developing trading strategies based on that data. 

First read trading-philosphy.md to understand the trading philosophy of the company, which is the basis for all the research and development we do in the company.

Then you can continue by reading our platform documentation, starting with `platform_docs/getting-started.md`, there might be some technical details about the internals but what you really want to focus on is understanding how you can use the platform to do your research.

Finally you can have a look at the `data` directory. That's where the historical data, the strategies, they testing results and all the research findings will be stored.

The engineering team has already tested the platform with some mock strategies, but there hasn't been a real research process yet, so there might be some missing features, or things that are not working as expected.

Your first task is to get familiar with the philosophy, the platform and the data, and generate a report on how would you organize the research process, what are the main steps, what are the main challenges you see, and how would you address those challenges. If you already detect weaknesses in the platform or missing features that would be critical for the research process, include them in the report as well and propose solutions for them.
