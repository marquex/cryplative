# Cryplative Trading Philosophy

Cryplative final goal is to grow our capital in an aggressive way, but we still need to define what "aggressive" means in our context, as we want to do it in a sustainable way that allows us to keep growing for a long time without taking too much risk.

In cryplative we want to approach the algorithmic trading problem with a scientific mindset. So the definition of aggresive growth should be measured in terms of profits, risk, and drawdown... We still need to find what are our target for those and related metrics, so we can optimize our strategies and decisions towards those targets.

Our decision should be based on data and evidence, and we should be always learning from our successes and failures, and iterating on our strategies based on what we learn. We want to research and experiment with different trading strategies in a systematic way, learning from our successes and failures, and iterating on our strategies based on what we learn.

## Trading restrictions

In cryplative we have some trading restrictions that will help us to reduce the research space:

* We use binance as our exchange, so we can only operate with pairs that are listed on binance.
* We are based in europe, so we can only operate with USDC pairs, as its the stablecoin that is accepted in our jurisdiction.
* We can only operate with spot trading, so we can only take long positions and we cannot use leverage.
* We are a slow trading company, our trading intervals are 1h, 4h, 1d and 1w. We are not doing high frequency trading and we are not taking advantage of short term market inefficiencies, but we are trying to find strategies that can perform well in the medium and long term.

USDC is not the most popular stablecoin, there is less number of pairs that we can operate with, less liquidity, and less historical data than using USDT, but it will also reduce the research space and allow us to focus on a smaller number of pairs. We can always use the equivalent USDT pair for the research to have more historical data and then implement the strategy in the USDC pair, as they should have similar behavior.

## Knowing the markets

Knowledge is power and the money is in the details, so we want to have a deep understanding of the markets we operate in, and the assets we trade.

We never develop a single pair strategy and try to make it work for all pairs, there is no such thing because every pair has its own characteristics and behaviors, so we want to know the specific characteristics of every pair we trade, and find out what are the best strategies for every pair based on them.

We only do technical research, we don't do fundamental research, so we need to gather all the historical data we can about the pairs we want to trade, the historical about the technical indicators, the historical about the market conditions, the historical about correlation with other pairs... We want to have a deep understanding of the historical behavior of the pairs we trade, and use that understanding to find patterns and strategies that can perform well in the future.

## We have a custom trading platform

We have developed a custom trading platform that allows us to implement and test our strategies in a systematic way, and also to execute our strategies in a reliable way.

We have a development team ready to improve the platform and implement new features based on the feedback from the research team, so we can have a platform that is tailored to our needs and allows us to implement and test our strategies in the best possible way.

If we feel that the platform is limiting our research in some way, we can always ask the development team to implement new features or improve existing ones, so we can have a platform that allows us to do the research we want to do in the best possible way. The phrase "We can't do that because our platform doesn't allow it" should never be an excuse for not doing something, we should always find a way to do it, either by improving the platform or by finding a workaround.


## Strategy development process

We want to generate a signal system that can give us a signal to buy or sell a pair based on the historical data and the technical indicators. Signal generators can be based on an infinite number of combinations of technical indicators, patterns, and market conditions, so we want to have a systematic way to research and test different signal generators based on the historical data and the technical indicators.

Some examples of data that we can use for the research are:

* Price data: open, close, high, low, volume...
* Technical indicators: RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands, Moving Averages...
* Market conditions: volatility, trend, correlation with other pairs...
* Patterns: support and resistance levels, chart patterns, candlestick patterns...
* General asset class conditions: Are the crypto markets in general a bull market, bear market, or sideways market?

We can use machine learning techniques to find patterns in the data and generate signals. We can also use more traditional techniques based on technical analysis and pattern recognition.

We can research the internet for existing strategies and new ways to get new data and combine technical indicators and patterns to generate signals.

We don't have limits on what kind of strategies we can research as long as they are based on data that can be obtained consistently and reliably, and that can be tested in a systematic way with our platform. 

Every strategy needs to be tested against every single pair, because every pair has its own characteristics and behaviors, so we want to find out what are the best strategies for every pair based on their specific characteristics. Also strategies work better on certain market conditions, we need to have a systematic way to evaluate strategies under different market conditions. That way we know how reliable is a signal for a specific pair and market condition, and we can use that signal to trade that pair in a more reliable way.

For every signal we generate we need to know:
* What's the expected return of that signal based on the historical data for the specific pair and market condition that we are evaluating it on.
* What's the reliability of that signal based on the historical data for the specific pair and market condition
* What's the drawdown of that signal based on the historical data for the specific pair and market condition

## Risk management

We don't have a specific risk management strategy defined yet, but we want to have a systematic way to evaluate the risk of our strategies and find ways to mitigate that risk. We want to find strategies that can perform well in the long term with a reasonable level of risk, so we can grow our capital in an aggressive but in a sustainable way.

Once we have a good collection of strategies that generate good signals for different pairs and market conditions, we want to find a way to combine those signals in a portfolio that can perform well in the long term with a reasonable level of risk. We want to find a way to allocate our capital among the different strategies and pairs in a way that maximizes our returns while minimizing our risk.
